<h1>LiFeLooP and sometimes KNiFeLooP</h1>
<h2>Live Improvised Sound Performance</h2>
<p>I want to explore the recycling of sound relaying, through contact microphones, the physical materials that I find in to a multi track looper and digital delay and back and forth through a mixer. A real time generation of musique concrete.  With this in mind the work process will be an open one. The frankness of the techniques shown in the performance enables the audience to easily understand the relationship between the physical and the sonic.</p>
<p>LifeLoop In KnifeMode
The noise of knives, soothing in an uncomfortable way, introduce the setting to an illusive form of seduction. That of the danger of the instrument. But contact mikes, along with various small instruments, (table harp, whistle..) and tapes of found sound, turn that danger into something not quite poetic, not quite harmonic, but nevertheless seductive. </p>

<h2>darkness and light intertwined</h2>
<p>
A generative audio and video installation with <a href="http://hexler.net/lab/oratory/" target="_blank">H3XL3R</a>.</p>
<p>
Am I attracted to this? Or disgusted by it? Does it arouse me sexually? Perversely? Or merely interest me mildly? Is there anybody watching me watching this? Monitoring my reactions. Determining my personal levels of perversion. What is happening exactly? How
does it end? Does it ever end?</p>
<p>
The dark and the light struggle on the screen. What is real? What is imagined? What is willed into reality by the imagination? What do you perceive? Are your eyes even open? Is the screen really on the wall? Or in your mind? This is an installation full of questions but offering no answers, only the voyeuristic peephole into another's creation.</p>

<h2>Return to Sender</h2>
<p>
Many radio receivers and two to four mini-FM transmitters, each transmitting on a different frequency what it is being fed or what it picks up from the near field chaos surrounding it. The transmission between the devices is affected by what frequency the receivers are tuned to, the immediate electro-magnetic field, human presence and furniture. The signal is not a sound loop travelling in the area of intervention, but a spontaneous event, resulting from collective action in perpetual movement.</p>

<h2>Infinite Loop Surveillance</h2>
<p>
Two tape loops run on four old reel to reel tape machines. While two of the machines are constantly recording the sound in the space with microphones, the other two devices simultaneously play back that recording, echoed, which is then also recorded, and so on.
This work is a play on the hypothesis that the apparently empty tape recordings may sometimes contain messages from the beyond. This is known as "Electronic Voice Phenomenon" or "Tonbandstimmen". It is a test to discover if there are any ghosts still lingering in the space.</p>

<h1>Li/kNiFeLoOp</h1>
<h2>Live Improvised Sound Performance</h2>
<p> Right now I am most interested in live-sampling and the recycling of sounds and noises, whether acoustic or electronic, the repetition and manipulation of these samples and the discovery of new sounds through this journey. I use knives, metal bowls, self-built electronics, tapes, a multi track looper and digital delay with a mixer and magnetic and contact microphones. A real time generation and mutilation of musique concrete. With this in mind the work process is an open one. The frankness of the techniques shown in the performance enables the audience to easily understand the relationship between the physical and the sonic. </p>
<p>Links to other audio and video documentation on <a href="http://www.archive.org/details/lifeloop" target="_blank">archive.org</a> and <a href="https://vimeo.com/knifeloop" target="_blank">Vimeo</a>. <br />
  Below are some examples of solo and collaborative live shows. They do not include the long term project with Joffrey Guillon, <a href="http://archive.org/search.php?query=creator%3A%22noisiV%22%20AND%20%28noisiv%29" target="_blank">noisiV</a></p>

<h2>ELECTRICITY</h2>
<p>Live A/V Performance with <a href="http://hexler.net/lab/oratory/" target="_blank">H3XL3R</a> <br />
  What is inaudible is made audible. What is invisible is made visible.<br />
  The sounds of elctro-magnetic fields around highly charged devices   are relayed, with the help of induction coil microphones, to a multi   track looper and digital delay where they are manipulated to resemble   coherent sound patterns. This enables the audience to find a relationship   to what had previously been inaudible. The effect is enhanced by giving   it life on a screen when feeding the audio signal into a custom GPU   software especially designed for on-the-fly visual synthesis. A visual   experience leaving you with the impression of having survived a lightning   storm.<br />
  With these tools the secret sound waves of different electric devices   are discovered and exploited and made visual in the moment. The source   materials from which the electromagnetic fields radiate are not all   that is important. The procedure that their sounds are put through in   the act of creation, sonification and impromptu re-interpretation on   the screen makes the A/V performance an intriguing insight into the   invisible, inaudible world around us.</p>

<h2>reel tape lie detector</h2>
<p> A number of old reel-to-reel machines are arranged in series with a tape running through from first to last machine. The first machine is recording through a mixer (with line inputs from the other machines) and the rest are playing the signal back thus creating an analogue delay effect that is infinitely looped over a large space and long period, normally resulting in a cacophony of semi-random sound. 
  LifeLoop records his voice, then re-records these passing signals again and again until the truth behind the words and noise can be heard. <br />
  This video is a part of the <a href="http://www.typeontour.com/TYPE_ON_TOUR/Lifeloop.html" target="_blank">TypeOnTour</a> event, a crossing of sound and typography. <br />
  &quot;For this event I used a speech to text software to visualise my texts and let the tape machines do their thing and see what kind of texts the software would interpret.&quot;</p>

<h2>Return to Sender</h2>
<p> Four radio receivers and two to four mini-FM transmitters, each transmitting on a different frequency what it is being fed or what it picks up from the near field chaos surrounding it. The transmission between the devices is affected by what frequency the receivers are tuned to, the immediate electro-magnetic field, human presence and furniture. The signal is not a sound loop travelling in the area of intervention, but a spontaneous event, resulting from collective action in perpetual movement.<br />

<h2>Radio</h2>
<p>Over the years I have been involved in many different temporary radio projects. Starting in 2002 with the <a href="http://www.noweb.org/noisiv/kampa/" target="_blank">Radiokampagne.de</a>, a now defunct association, there was a push to achieve a real political change in the local media laws of Berlin. Well..... we are still trying. Here are some examples of past temporary projects that I was involved with. <a href="http://www.kiosk-berlin.de/deutsch/radio.htm" target="_blank">Ersatzradio</a> (2003), <a href="http://www.ausland-berlin.de/fls/performance/radiodoku.pdf" target="_blank">RadioRiff</a> (2003/4), <a href="http://www.radiopiloten.de/herbstradio" target="_blank">radioherbst</a> (2005),  Funkwelle (2008), <a href="http://www.herbstradio.org/" target="_blank">Herbstradio</a>  (2009) and <a href="http://piradio.de/news/2010-08-radio-einheit-991" target="_blank">Radio Einheit</a> (2010).<br />
  And now? Right now there is a weak transmission on 88.4FM that is divided out between ten independant groups. <a href="http://piradio.de/" target="_blank">Pi-Radio</a></a>, <a href="http://freiesradiopotsdam.wordpress.com/" target="_blank"> Frrapo</a> in Potsdam, <a href="http://www.studioansage.de/" target="_blank">Studio Ansage</a> and <a href="http://senderberlin.org/colaboradio/" target="_blank">Colaboradio</a> (the group I am working with) make up four of the ten groups with blocks of time that are actually working together towards something. This is a kind of experiment with community radio, although it is a long way away from a real Free Radio as outlined in the radiokampagne link above. These four make up the potential for a future in this direction at least. Let's see where they take us. </p>

<h2>Radio Tesla</h2>
<p><span lang="en" xml:lang="en">RADIO TESLA presented selected programs, fragments or mental leaps from the past, present and future of radio. An invitation to address the artistic, cultural and historical dimensions and possibilities of the medium. Every month there was a theme and every Wednesday an event presenting that theme in the rotating radio genres feature, radio play, radio art and borders. Every fifth Wednesday of the month featured a bonus track. After two and a half years (2005-7) at <a href="http://www.tesla-berlin.de/page76.html" target="_blank">TESLA</a> media art laboratory RadioTesla moved to the venue <a href="http://www.ausland-berlin.de/radio-tesla" target="_blank">Ausland</a> for a monthly radio listening lounge for 2008.<br />
  One of the highlights of all these radio listening events was the festival and prolog <a href="http://www.tesla-berlin.de/page335.html" target="_blank">Radiovisionen</a>. The Radiovisionen Festival looked back on the origins and into a   fictitious future of the medium: What does radio sound like in 150   years? In which social and political context will radio exist?.   Radiovisionen are Science Fiction.</span></p>
